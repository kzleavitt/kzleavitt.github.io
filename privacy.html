Privacy policy

Last updated October 1, 2026

Hermes is a personal assistant used only by its owner, Kurt Leavitt. It is not a public service.

What data it accesses

With the owner's permission, Hermes accesses the owner's own Google account data (such as Gmail, Google Calendar, Contacts, Drive, Docs and Sheets) through Google's APIs.

How the data is used

The data is used only to provide the owner with summaries, reminders, drafts and similar personal assistance. It is processed on the owner's own computer.

Sharing

The data is not sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising. Hermes's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Removing access

Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Contact

Questions can be sent to the owner through github.com/kzleavitt.
